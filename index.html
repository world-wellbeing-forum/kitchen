Editorial notes & references

Sources and collection standards

World Wellbeing Kitchen is a curated collection of vegetarian and vegan recipe adaptations. Recipes written specifically for this collection are identified as editorial adaptations; reference links below inform the broader plant-based cooking, nutrition and food-safety approach. A link is not a claim that a recipe was copied from that source. Nutrition values and appliance timings are estimates unless independently tested and analysed.

  1. ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition — Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2024): food groups, pulses, whole grains, vegetables and cooking practices.
  2. U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Practical Plant-Based Cooking (2023): plant-based meal ideas and practical cooking guidance.
  3. Food Safety and Standards Authority of India — The Pink Book: Your Guide for Safe and Nutritious Food at Home: safe purchasing, storage, preparation, cooking and leftovers.
  4. U.S. Food and Drug Administration — Safe Food Handling: safe handling, refrigeration and reheating practices.
  5. Penn State Extension — Key Food Safety Instructions for Recipes (updated 2024): practical recipe-writing food-safety prompts.

Earlier entries in this collection were drafted as original recipe concepts and were not based on a documented external source list at the time they were created. We are adding transparent references now; we will not invent citations retroactively or attribute original wording to sources that were not used. Recipe attribution and inspiration will be added individually whenever a recipe is adapted from a named source.
