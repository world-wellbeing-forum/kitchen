# World Wellbeing Kitchen

**By Sadhna Dixit**  
An initiative of **World Wellbeing Forum**

Traditional Wisdom. Modern Nutrition. A Healthier World.

A growing vegetarian and vegan recipe collection focused on enjoyable everyday cooking, plant-forward protein, fibre and practical kitchen methods.

## Current website
The cookbook and website are being developed as a 300-recipe journey through **all 28 Indian states and 8 union territories**, with dedicated city collections for Hyderabad, Bengaluru and Delhi. The regional development map is maintained in `research/india-regional-recipe-map.json`.

The collection will represent regional home cooking, festive dishes, street-food favourites and everyday meals. Examples include Punjabi sarson da saag, Haryanvi bajra dishes, Uttar Pradesh nimona and bedmi-aloo, Hyderabadi bagara baingan, Kerala avial, Tamil Nadu sambar and pongal, Kashmiri dum aloo, Goan vegetable xacuti, Maharashtrian misal and pithla, Gujarati undhiyu, Assamese vegetable preparations and Bengali shukto and aloo posto.

The 300-recipe collection is being built as individual recipe files. A regional dish listed in the development map is a research direction, not a claim that its recipe, photograph or testing is already complete.

## Recipe standards
Each completed recipe is intended to include:
- Ingredients and quantities
- Serving size and preparation, cooking and total kitchen time
- Step-by-step stovetop method
- Microwave, conventional oven and air-fryer alternatives where suitable
- Vegetarian or vegan designation
- Estimated nutrition information

Appliance timings are starting estimates until tested with specific appliances. Nutrition values are estimates, not laboratory analyses. This site is educational and is not medical advice.

## Dietary labels
Vegan recipes contain no animal-derived ingredients. Vegetarian recipes may include dairy and contain no meat, fish or eggs. Check packaged ingredient labels for hidden animal-derived ingredients.
